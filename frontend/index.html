End-to-End Cryptographic Architecture

A trustless 4-step pipeline: real economic facts originate on source chains, attest via Merkle proofs, and deterministically mint borrow capacity on Creditcoin.

01

Source Economic Fact

A merchant emits a real payment or invoice settlement on Ethereum Sepolia via EconomicEvents.sol. No trusted oracle or private database.

02

Attestcoin Verification

AttestcoinVerifier.sol validates Merkle inclusion & EIP-658 receipt status directly against Creditcoin's native 0xFD2 precompile.

03

Sovereign Credit Passport

CreditPassport.sol permanently accumulates verified volume and repayments. CreditEngine.sol computes mathematically bounded capacity.

04

Funded Capital Draw

PolicyEngine.sol enforces hard risk ceilings ($2k max). CreditLine.sol disburses native tCTC directly from the funded liquidity pool.

AttestcoinVerifier extracts merchantId, amount, and receipt success directly on-chain from proven RLP bytes. A relayer cannot manipulate transaction metadata.
